# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## What to Build
A complete visual and structural overhaul of an existing developer/designer portfolio application. The core content (experience, skills, projects) remains unchanged, but the user interface must be rebuilt from the ground up to reflect a premium, cinematic, "Awwwards-level" standard. The project also requires the creation of a new, distinct brand logo and favicon.

## Target Users
- **Recruiters & Hiring Managers:** Need to quickly parse Android/Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and AI/ML skills, experience metrics (e.g. 58% crash reduction, 90% NLP accuracy), and contact information without friction.
- **Investors & Technical Leads:** Need to judge full-stack and mobile competence (Room, SQL, Python, LangChain, React) and design taste through the quality, motion, and layout of the site itself.

## Features of the App
### Must-Have:
- **Cinematic Hero Section:** High-impact initial viewport framing Nisarg as a results-driven Android & AI Developer.
- **Gapless Bento Grid:** For displaying deep technical skills (Kotlin, Python, LangChain, Cloud) and Certifications (NLP Python Certificate).
- **Rich Motion (GSAP):** Horizontal accordions and scroll-stacking for showcasing 2.5+ years of experience and academic projects.
- **Distinct Sections:** About Me, Tech Stack, Experience (Billiyo Health, Nyusoft), Projects (E-Shark, AI Chess Bot), Certifications, Leadership.
- **New Brand Assets:** Logo and Favicon.
- **Fully Responsive Design:** Perfect scaling from mobile to ultra-wide desktop.

### Nice-to-Have:
- Dark/Light mode toggle (if it aligns with the final design system).
- Interactive project galleries.
