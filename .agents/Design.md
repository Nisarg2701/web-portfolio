# Design System & Page Plan

## 1. PRE-FLIGHT <design_plan>
- **Python RNG Execution:** 
  - Hero: Cinematic Center
  - Typography: Cabinet Grotesk (Subject to User Choice)
  - Components: Inline Typography Images, Horizontal Accordions, Infinite Marquee
  - GSAP Paradigms: Card Stacking, Image Scale & Fade Scroll
- **Core Dials:** DESIGN_VARIANCE: 5 | MOTION_INTENSITY: 9 | VISUAL_DENSITY: 5
- **AIDA Check:**
  - *Attention:* Cinematic Center Hero. Massive width `max-w-6xl`, max 2-3 lines of text.
  - *Interest:* Gapless Bento Grid showing Android/AI Skills + `NLP_IN_Python_certificate.jpg`.
  - *Desire:* Horizontal Accordions and Card Stacking for Experience (Billiyo Health, Nyusoft) & Projects (E-Shark, AI Chess Bot).
  - *Action:* Massive contrast CTA for Contact/Resume Download.

## 2. Colour and Theme
- **Theme Locked:** Dark Tech & AI Native.
- **Palette:** Off-black backgrounds (`bg-zinc-950` / `bg-black`), dark charcoal surface layers, with **Emerald Green** as the single high-contrast accent color for buttons, hovers, and highlights.
- Ensure button contrast passes WCAG AA (e.g., dark text on Emerald buttons, or Emerald text with Emerald borders for ghost buttons).

## 3. Fonts and Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Geist (Sans) + JetBrains Mono (for code snippets, eyebrows, and technical data).
- **Display Scale:** `text-4xl` to `text-6xl`, tracking tight, leading none.
- **Constraints:** Maximum 2-3 lines for H1. No 6-line wraps. Italic descender clearance required.

## 4. Assets
- **Resume Data:** Deep integration of 2.5+ years Android dev, Jetpack Compose, Python/NLP metrics (60% performance optimizations, 90% NLP accuracy).
- **Certificate Image:** Placed natively in the Bento Grid without generic card framing.
- Needs specific Logo and Favicon designed for the brand.
